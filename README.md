### *(Team Project: Food.com Recipe Popularity Analysis | 2025)*


### **Project Context**

This personal README summarizes our team work and learning outcomes within our team project analyzing the **Food.com Recipes and Reviews Dataset**.
Our collective goal was to explore which recipe attributes — nutritional balance, effort level, and cultural background — contribute most to user popularity and satisfaction.

---
### **Files in This Repository**
-B02-Uncovering-What-Makes-A-Dish-Popular.ipynb

---

### **What We Learned**

* Strengthened our ability to handle **large datasets** (numeric + text).
* Learned to combine **Exploratory Data Analysis** with **user-behavior interpretation**, moving from statistics to real-world meaning.
* Improved proficiency in **Python** and **data storytelling**.
* Gained a deeper appreciation for **data ethics and representativeness**, understanding how missingness and imbalance can distort results.

---

### **Challenges We Overcame**

* Managing heavy missing values and duplicated data during merging.
* Identifying which variables were truly useful for modeling versus those creating noise.
* Cleaning and structuring unorganized ingredient content to extract meaningful signals.

---

### **Key Insight from Analysis**

 Recipes that combine **moderate calorie levels**, **balanced nutrition**, and **positive sensory descriptions** consistently achieve the highest ratings.
 This finding reinforces how data can reflect human behavior — people prefer dishes that feel both **achievable and enjoyable**.
